What this number does and does not include

Included

Aircraft rental with fuel, and flight plus ground instruction at the rates you set. Ground school materials come free with enrollment, and simulator hours are free for enrolled students.

Optional and third-party

Nothing is left out of the estimate itself. The written test, the examiner’s checkride fee, your medical exam and personal equipment all sit in the checklist above, so you can include or exclude each one.

This tool is an estimate only. Final costs are subject to variation based on aircraft type, instructor rates, and individual student performance.
