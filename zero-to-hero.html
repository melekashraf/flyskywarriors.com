Most schools quote you flight time and let everything else arrive later as invoices. Our Commercial, CFI and CFII phases each include 25 to 30 hours of one-on-one ground instruction, written exam preparation, oral preparation, lesson plan development and maneuver briefings, built into the price rather than billed on top of it.
